I am Ryo Emori, an astrophysics graduate student working on the first galaxies, extremely metal-poor systems, and early black hole growth. I mainly use JWST and Subaru/HSC imaging and spectroscopy to study these galaxies.

## Research interests
- Population III / first galaxies
- Extremely metal-poor galaxies (EMPGs)
- Early black hole growth and reionization-era galaxies
- JWST (NIRCam/NIRSpec) and Subaru/HSC imaging & spectroscopy
